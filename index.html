SecretTransport

Turn any text into an image you can paste into a message or email. Only someone with the passphrase can turn it back. Everything happens in your browser: nothing is uploaded.

Or read the text from a picture:
The picture itself is encrypted, not its text.

Paste an image here (Ctrl/Cmd+V), drop files, or . A split secret needs all its images.

AES-256-GCM, with the key derived from your passphrase by Argon2id (64 MiB of memory per guess, so mass guessing is expensive). The image proves nothing about its content, but its size hints at the message length. Anyone who copies the image can try guesses offline, and nothing in a web page can limit that: a long, random passphrase is your real protection. Repeated wrong guesses on this device are also slowed down.
